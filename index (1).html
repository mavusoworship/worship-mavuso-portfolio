About

I enjoy finding ways technology can help organisations work better.

I'm a BCom Information Systems graduate from the University of Fort Hare, skilled in applying technology to solve business challenges. I'm interested in understanding how organisations work and finding ways technology can make them more efficient.

I kept an academic average above 75% throughout my degree and received top-student awards in Computer Literacy and Databases. In 2025, I achieved the second-highest results in my department, and I am expecting to graduate Cum Laude in May 2027.

My academic projects include the Forte Auto Repair & Parts Centre Information System, where I led an eight-member team as project manager to design a digital system for a paper-based workshop. I also helped design and build the SRC Student Safety Management System, which improved incident reporting on campus; the Bantu Community Service web platform, which connects rural residents with local service providers; and Digit Detective, a mobile game built in C# and .NET MAUI. In my own time, I built Sovereign 10, a real-time strategy game with AI opponents, which is live online.

Since 2024, I've tutored Computer Literacy, Databases, Fundamentals of Information Systems and Essentials of IT. The role has strengthened my communication skills, taught me to work with people from different backgrounds, and given me responsibility for others' progress, all of which I'll bring into any team.

I'm looking for graduate and internship opportunities that give me exposure to a professional environment and hands-on experience, while allowing me to add real value to a team.

Projects

01 — Personal project · 2026

Sovereign 10

A real-time strategy game inspired by Risk, where you lead a real country against nine AI nations whose army sizes are based on real 2026 population data.

  • Each AI opponent has its own personality, such as aggressive, defensive or opportunistic, works out the odds before every battle, and explains its decisions in plain language.
  • The Google Gemini API writes live battle commentary and an end-of-war newspaper report, and anyone with a link can watch a game live.
  • Secured with password hashing and server-side checks on every move, backed by 180 automated tests and deployed on Render.
